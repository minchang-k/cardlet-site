Short version

Super Card edits your card-news and carousel images on your phone. We do not collect, receive, store or share your photos, projects or personal data. There is no account, no analytics and no ads.

What the app does with your data

  • Photos you pick. The Android system photo picker gives the app only the photos you select. The app has no access to the rest of your photos.
  • Projects. Your projects (text, settings and copies of the photos you used) are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes them.
  • Saving and sharing. When you tap Save, images are saved to Pictures/Super Card on your phone. When you tap Share, the images go to the app you choose; that app's own policy then applies.
  • Fonts. When you choose a font that is not bundled, the app downloads the font file from a public font host (e.g. Google Fonts' GitHub repository). This request contains no personal data, only the usual network information any download carries (such as your IP address), which the host may log under its own policy.
  • Purchases. The one-time Pro purchase is handled by Google Play. We receive only the purchase confirmation needed to unlock Pro; we never see your payment details. Pro is tied to the device it was bought on: to do this without any server, the app passes Google Play a one-way hash of a device identifier (it cannot be turned back into the identifier and is not linked to your name or account by us).

What we do NOT do

  • No personal data collection, no server that receives your content.
  • No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties; no training of models on your content.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
